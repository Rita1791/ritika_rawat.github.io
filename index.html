When evolutionary conservation becomes evidence.

The TP53 project investigates whether cancer-associated mutation hotspots exhibit stronger evolutionary conservation than surrounding regions. The analysis combines comparative sequence data with human cancer mutation information to examine the relationship between evolutionary constraint and mutation susceptibility.

6/6 hotspots showed conservation across the analysed mammalian sequences.
Mean hotspot conservation was approximately 1.000, compared with approximately 0.934 for the selected DBD controls.
Statistical comparison: U = 819, p = 0 in the reported analysis.
